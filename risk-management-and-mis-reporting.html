Enterprise Risk Management & Performance Intelligence

Effective risk management and performance measurement drive sustainable growth. AVKAS & Co. implements holistic Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) frameworks to identify, assess and mitigate risks across the organization. We also leverage performance intelligence using data analytics and dashboards to monitor key financial and operational metrics in real time. This dual focus ensures that strategic objectives are met while safeguarding against unforeseen threats.

Our ERM services integrate global best practices (such as COSO, ISO 31000) with industry-specific insights. We help clients establish risk governance structures (risk committees, policies) and embed a risk aware culture. On the performance side, our team designs intuitive management reports and KPI scorecards that provide clarity to executives. With predictive analytics and benchmarking, clients can spot trends, anticipate market changes and make agile decisions. By combining risk insight with operational data, AVKAS & Co. empowers organizations to pursue growth confidently and resiliently.

Our Range of Services:

  • Enterprise Risk Assessments and Framework Design
  • Internal Control Reviews and Strengthening
  • Compliance Risk (Regulatory/Governance) Evaluation
  • Key Performance Indicator (KPI) Development and Dashboards
  • Data Analytics for Trend Analysis and Forecasting
  • Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Planning
  • Cybersecurity Risk Advisory
  • Fraud Risk Management and Preventive Controls
