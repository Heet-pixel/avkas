Corporate Law & Governance Compliance

Strong corporate governance and compliance with legal frameworks are vital for sustainable success. AVKAS & Co. advises on corporate law and governance matters to protect companies and stakeholders. From incorporation to board advisory, we ensure that entities meet statutory requirements under the Companies Act, SEBI regulations and other applicable laws. Good governance also means embedding best practices such as risk oversight and ethical standards at the board and management levels.

Our seasoned legal team and company secretaries translate complex regulations into clear guidance. We help structure organizations, draft and review policies (like board charters, CSR strategies, insider trading codes), and prepare mandatory filings (annual returns, board resolutions, etc.). By staying attuned to legislative changes, we help clients anticipate and adapt to new obligations. The result is a governance framework that supports transparency and investor confidence, while minimizing legal risks.

Our Range of Services:

  • Company Formation and Restructuring (Domestic & Foreign Entities)
  • Board and Shareholder Governance Advisory
  • Compliance Monitoring (MCA, SEBI, RBI filings)
  • Corporate Secretarial Services and Statutory Registers
  • SEBI (Listing) and Stock Exchange Compliance
  • Regulatory and Policy Advisory (Corporate Governance Code, etc.)
  • Legal Support for Mergers, Acquisitions and Restructurings
  • Drafting of Agreements, Resolutions and Compliance Manuals
