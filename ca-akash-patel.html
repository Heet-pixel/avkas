CA Akash Patel

B.Com, ISA (ICAI), ACA

Managing Director

CA Akash Patel is a Chartered Accountant with over five years’ specialised experience in accounting, taxation and dispute resolution. A commerce graduate with legal training (LLB) and DISA accreditation, Akash has a strong track record in finalising books and managing statutory compliance for sectors including manpower services, food chains, manufacturing, professional services and real estate.

Experience

A seasoned tax controversy professional, Akash handles income-tax and GST scrutiny assessments, appeals and representation before authorities. He is well-versed in the full suite of direct and indirect tax laws — TDS, VAT, Service Tax, Excise and GST — and applies this knowledge to design defensible positions that minimise fiscal exposure. His assurance portfolio includes internal, government, bank and insurance audits across manufacturing, trading and service entities. Akash also brings niche expertise in real-estate accounting and regulatory compliance (including RERA), and has served as a forensic auditor in Income-Tax Department search & seizure matters — delivering data-driven evidence analysis and supporting complex investigations. Clients value his methodical case preparation, clear stakeholder communication and focus on sustainable remediation.
