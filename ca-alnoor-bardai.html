CA Alnoor Bardai

B.Com, ACA

Managing Director

CA Alnoor Bardai is a Chartered Accountant with a strong foundation in audit, taxation and compliance, advising clients across real estate, co-operative and service sectors with international exposure to statutory filings and compliance assurance.

Experience

He specialises in income-tax and indirect tax matters and is experienced in handling scrutiny assessments and compliance reviews. Alnoor’s audit experience includes internal, statutory and co-operative society audits, where he focuses on control enhancement and accurate financial reporting. He also has hands-on knowledge of real-estate accounting and regulatory frameworks, including RERA-related compliance. Practical, thorough and client-centric, Alnoor is known for his attention to detail, effective coordination with regulatory bodies and ability to convert audit observations into pragmatic remediation plans.
