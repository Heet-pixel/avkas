Project Finance & Infrastructure Advisory

Infrastructure and large-capital projects require specialized financial structuring and advisory. AVKAS & Co. assists clients in planning, funding and executing projects. We conduct feasibility studies, financial modeling and risk assessments to ensure projects are bankable. Whether governments, developers or financial institutions, our clients gain clarity on financing options (debt, equity, PPP), subsidies and risk-sharing mechanisms that drive project viability.

Our experts have a deep understanding of infrastructure sectors and funding sources. We craft innovative financing structures, negotiate with lenders or investors, and advise on regulatory compliance (permits, environmental norms, PPP guidelines). By coordinating technical, legal and financial inputs, we help secure funding on optimal terms. Clients appreciate our pragmatic yet strategic approach, we work to close financing gaps, optimize capital costs and ensure that project timelines and returns align with stakeholder objectives.

Our Range of Services:

  • Financial Feasibility Studies and Project Modeling
  • Debt Syndication and Fundraising (Bank/Non-Bank Lenders, Multilaterals)
  • Public Private Partnership (PPP) Structuring and Bid Advisory
  • Capital Budgeting and Cash Flow Optimization
  • Infrastructure Transaction Advisory and Due Diligence
  • Risk Analysis and Mitigation Strategies for Projects
  • Consortium Banking Finance (Single project loan by multiple banks)
  • Support for Government Project Approvals and Concessions
