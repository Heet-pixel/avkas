Business & Asset Valuation Services

Accurate valuations are critical for informed decision-making in financing, M&A, compliance and strategic planning. AVKAS & Co. provides objective business and asset valuation to ensure transparency in transactions and reporting. We apply globally recognised valuation standards, analysing economic drivers and industry trends to determine fair value.

Accurate valuations are critical for informed decision-making in financing, M&A, compliance and strategic planning. We apply recognised valuation methodologies – income, market and asset based – and document our assumptions clearly. Whether for a start-up investment round, family succession plan or financial reporting, our valuations give stakeholders the clarity they need.

Our Range of Services:

  • Business and Enterprise Valuation
  • Valuation of Shares and Securities
  • Valuation of Intangible Assets and Brands
  • Purchase Price Allocation and Impairment Testing
