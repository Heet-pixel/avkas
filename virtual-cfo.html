Virtual CFO

A virtual Chief Financial Officer provides strategic financial leadership without the overhead of a full-time executive. AVKAS & Co. offers Virtual CFO services to deliver high-level financial expertise on demand. Our approach brings “the same high-level financial strategy and experience as a top-level, in-house CFO,” but with greater flexibility and cost efficiency. This solution is ideal for growing companies that need sophisticated financial guidance—such as forecasting, capital raising and profitability analysis—but aren’t ready for a permanent CFO hire.

Our Virtual CFOs work as embedded members of your team, building trust and continuity over time. We oversee all finance functions: strategic planning, budget management, cash flow optimisation and investor relations. By providing monthly or quarterly reviews, we help leaders understand business drivers and make better decisions. Clients can also tap into our broader network of finance professionals; in effect, our virtual CFOs bring a whole team’s worth of expertise at a fraction of the cost.

Our Range of Services:

  • Financial Strategy and Annual Operating Plan
  • Cash Flow and Working Capital Management
  • MIS, Board and Investor Reporting
  • Fund-raising and Banker Relations
  • Unit Economics and Pricing Analysis
  • Finance Team Hiring and Mentoring
