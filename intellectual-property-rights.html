Trademark & Intellectual Property Rights

Your brand and intangible assets are among your most valuable resources. Trademarks and other IP rights protect the goodwill you’ve built and enable global expansion. By securing trademarks, patents, and copyrights, businesses safeguard their reputation and create assets that support future growth. AVKAS & Co. helps clients understand and leverage IP as strategic assets in a global market.

We offer end-to-end IP support, grounded in deep legal and commercial insight. Our team manages the entire trademark and IP lifecycle – from searches and registration to portfolio management and enforcement – across multiple jurisdictions. Clients benefit from our proactive strategies for brand protection, including monitoring for infringements and advising on licensing or monetisation. With AVKAS & Co., organisations confidently expand their brands and preserve value in an increasingly knowledge-driven economy.

Our Range of Services:

  • Trademark Search, Filing and Registration
  • Copyright and Design Registration
  • Brand Portfolio Management and Renewals
  • Opposition, Rectification and Infringement Matters
  • IP Licensing and Assignment Agreements
