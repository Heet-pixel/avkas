Auditing and Assurance

Auditing and assurance services form the bedrock of corporate trust, transparency and sound governance. In today’s complex regulatory environment, regular financial audits ensure that stakeholders from investors to regulators have confidence in your reported results. AVKAS & Co. treats every audit as a strategic exercise: beyond verifying compliance, we use the audit process to uncover operational insights, strengthen controls, and identify opportunities for efficiency. By transforming audit findings into actionable intelligence, our approach helps organizations manage risk proactively and create value.

We combine deep industry expertise with advanced analytics and technology to deliver high quality, efficient assurance. Our independent perspective and commitment to integrity bolster stakeholder confidence while reducing the burden on management. Through continuous dialogue with your team, we pinpoint potential issues early and recommend practical improvements. Clients benefit from an audit that goes beyond the checklist one that provides clear insights for strategy, improves internal controls and aligns with global best practices.

Key Deliverables & Capabilities

  • Statutory Financial Statement Audits (IFRS/Ind AS, GAAP)
  • Internal and SOX Compliance Audits
  • IT Systems, Cybersecurity and Technology Assurance
  • ESG and Sustainability Reporting Assurance
  • Special-Purpose and Compliance Audits (e.g. process reviews)
  • Audit Committee & Board Governance Advisory
