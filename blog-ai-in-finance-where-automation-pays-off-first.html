AI in Finance: Where Automation Pays Off First

Technology12 July 2026By CA Devansh Shah5 min read

Finance transformation does not need a multi-year ERP programme. The best early wins are narrow, repetitive and measurable.

Start with reconciliations

Bank, intercompany and vendor reconciliations are rule-driven and high-volume – ideal for automation and anomaly detection.

Automate reporting workflows

Power BI and workflow tools can replace manual MIS packs. In one regional rollout, automating invoicing and reporting reclaimed around 1,500 working hours a year.

Keep humans on judgement

Let automation prepare, and let finance professionals review exceptions and write the commentary that explains the numbers.
