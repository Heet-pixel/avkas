Integrated Business Accounting & Financial Reporting

Efficient accounting and reporting provide the foundation for sound management and global compliance. AVKAS & Co. offers integrated accounting solutions that combine people, process and technology. We implement best-in-class accounting systems and policies so that clients have real-time visibility into their finances. By aligning financial processes with recognised standards (such as Ind AS/IFRS), our services ensure accuracy and consistency across global operations.

We act as an extension of our clients’ finance teams, leveraging automation and expertise to streamline routine tasks. Our professionals maintain up-to-date books, prepare consolidated financial statements and manage statutory reporting, freeing clients to focus on growth. We proactively address gaps and introduce data-driven dashboards, improving decision support. Companies benefit from a fully supported accounting cycle, from transactional bookkeeping to high-level financial analysis, all delivered with the reliability of a global practice.

Our Range of Services:

  • Bookkeeping, Ledger Maintenance and Closing
  • Preparation of Financial Statements (Ind AS / IFRS)
  • Consolidation and Group Reporting
  • GST, TDS and Statutory Return Filing
  • Outsourced Accounting and MIS
  • Accounting Policy and Standards Advisory
