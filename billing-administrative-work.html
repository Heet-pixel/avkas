Billing & Administrative Work

Efficient back-office operations are critical to keep businesses running smoothly. AVKAS & Co. offers reliable billing and administrative support to handle routine but essential tasks, so management can focus on growth. With standardised processes and timely reconciliations, we ensure that financial transactions are recorded accurately and on time.

Our administrative services are delivered by qualified professionals who follow standardised processes. We implement transparent billing systems and maintain organised records, giving clients visibility into receivables and payables. Timely reports and reconciliation help optimise working capital. The continuity and reliability of our support staff mean that no billing or HR matter fails through the cracks. Overall, clients gain cost-efficiency and the peace of mind that routine administrative tasks are in capable hands.

Key Deliverables & Capabilities

  • Invoice Generation, Delivery and Payment Follow-Up
  • Financial Record Maintenance (ledgers, documents)
  • Accounts Payable Processing and Vendor Management
  • Administrative Support (documentation, filing, correspondence)
  • Billing Reporting and Reconciliation
  • Customer Billing Inquiries and Helpdesk Support
  • Payroll Processing and Employee Data Management
