CA Akshat Shah

B.Com, M.Com, ACA

Managing Director

CA Akshat Shah is a dynamic, forward-thinking Chartered Accountant with international exposure and a strong focus on taxation, litigation, financial reporting and digital audits. Having worked with Grant Thornton Kenya, Akshat brings hands-on experience in cross-border taxation, IFRS/Ind-AS reporting and multinational compliance — equipping him to advise clients on global expansion and complex international structuring.

Experience

In practice, Akshat combines independent advisory experience with technology-led audit capabilities. He specialises in tax advisory, litigation support, IFRS conversions and technology-enabled assurance (digital audit frameworks and data analytics). Active involvement with WIRC Ahmedabad keeps him current on regulatory developments and industry trends. Known for an analytical, solution-driven mindset and disciplined execution, Akshat collaborates with multidisciplinary teams to deliver integrated compliance and advisory solutions tailored to modern business needs, while maintaining the highest standards of quality.
